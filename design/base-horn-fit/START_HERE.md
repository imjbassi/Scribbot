# One small gear: unpowered horn fit check

Print ONLY 01_motor_gear_fit.stl. This is the candidate base-drive pinion, not a release of the complete base. Your double-ended white horn is accepted as fitting your black MG90S shaft, based on your confirmation.

## Print

Use the Bambu A1 mini and the same PLA/profile used for the successful fit coupons: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.12 mm layers, 0.2 mm first layer. Print flat, at 100% scale, with 4 walls, 5 top/bottom layers and 40% infill. No supports. Remove any brim from teeth and slots. This fit result will apply to this FDM process; resin needs its own fit validation.

## Check without screws or power

1. Leave the servo unplugged. Begin with the horn loose, off the servo.
2. Put the horn against the underside of the gear, with the socket that fits the servo pointing away from the gear. Center the horn's center hole in the gear's large center opening.
3. Align the horn arms with one pair of opposite radial slots. Look through the slots: at least one horn hole on EACH arm must be fully visible. Choose approximately equal distances from the center. You do not need to measure or mark anything.
4. Check whether the gear rests flat and centered against the horn arms. If the hub prevents it sitting flat, stop and report that; do not sand or force the horn.
5. Seat the horn back on the disconnected servo, gently, and hold the gear in place. Check whether the original center screw is accessible through the large gear opening and whether the gear clears the servo case. Do not turn the gear to force the servo shaft.

Reply: "holes line up, sits flat, clears case" or identify which check fails. A photo is useful if something does not line up.

Do not power this loose assembly. No attachment screws are selected yet: the slots are nominally 2.2 mm wide, but this does NOT prove the horn holes accept M2 screws. Screw diameter/length and underside clearance must be matched before fastening. Do not enlarge the horn holes or use glue.
