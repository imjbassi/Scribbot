# Shoulder horn fit check (unpowered)

Print ONLY `01_shoulder_hub.stl`: one copy, flat as exported, PLA, 0.12 mm layers, 4 walls, 40% infill, no supports.

This is the real shoulder drive hub, not a throwaway coupon. It bolts to the MG996R's double-arm horn. Later, the upper arm bolts to it with four M3 screws into the hex nut pockets on its underside.

## Check (servo unplugged, no screws yet)

1. Hold the horn under the hub, with the **hex pockets facing the horn**. Center the horn's middle screw in the hub's big center hole.
2. Turn the horn until its two arms line up under one pair of **opposite slots**. On each arm, at least one horn hole should be fully visible through the slot, at about the same distance from the center on both sides.
3. The hub should sit **flat** on the horn, not rocking.
4. Put the horn back on the MG996R shaft (gently, don't twist the shaft) and hold the hub on top.
   - Can you reach the horn's center screw through the hub's center hole?
   - Does the hub clear the servo case all the way around?

## Report

"holes line up, sits flat, clears case", or which check fails. A photo helps if something is off.

Also tell me whether the MG996R came with any other horns (round disc, cross, single arm), and what small screws came with it.

## Later (not yet)

- Horn to hub: two of the horn's small supplied screws, down through the slots. The counterbores leave 2.5 mm of plastic under the screw heads, so short screws reach.
- Upper arm to hub: four M3 screws into M3 nuts pressed into the hex pockets.
