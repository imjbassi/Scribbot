# Camera stand

Holds the Logitech Brio 101 about 19 cm above a sticky note, looking straight down. See `camera_stand_diagram.png`.

## Print (PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, no supports)

| # | File | How it sits on the bed |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | `21_foot.stl` | flat |
| 22 | `22_column.stl` | standing up |
| 23 | `23_coupler.stl` | standing up |
| 24 | `24_top_arm.stl` | lying on its side, as exported |

## Hardware

- 4 x M3 x 12 screws. They cut their own thread in the column, so no nuts.
- 2 zip ties.

## Build

1. Push the column (22) into the foot socket. Screw one M3 through the socket hole.
2. Slide the coupler (23) over the top of the column. Screw in the lower hole.
3. Push the top arm (24) into the coupler, with the arm pointing out over the note pocket. Screw in the upper hole.
4. Open the webcam clip and hook it over the END of the arm, so the camera hangs under the arm with the lens pointing down. Zip-tie the clip through the two slots.
5. Plug the webcam into the PC.

Set the stand beside the arm, at least 30 cm away, so the arm can never reach it.
