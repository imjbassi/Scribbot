# Servo mounting-pattern fit templates

Print one copy of each STL. These are thin fit gauges, not operational brackets.

## Settings

Use your tested A1 mini setup: 0.4 mm nozzle, Generic PLA profile, 0.12 mm layers and 0.20 mm first layer. Print flat, labels up, 100% scale, no supports or raft. All islands must remain at their original scale if rearranged. MG90S layout is 82 x 83 mm; MG996R layout is 128 x 168 mm, within a 180 mm square bed. If a brim exceeds the slicer's allowed area, omit it or rearrange the individual pieces. Do not print both complete layouts together unless rearranged to fit.

Each STL contains multiple separate labeled plates: five MG90S options and nine MG996R options. Keep labels readable; remove loose strings without enlarging the holes.

## How to test

1. Keep the servo unplugged and remove any output horn that obstructs handling. Do not remove its case screws.
2. Feed the cable connector through the large rectangular opening first. Lower the servo body into that opening from the labeled side, so its mounting tabs rest on the plate.
3. Gently center the body in the opening. Its clearance is intentional; do not push it to one end to make only one hole align.
4. Hold the plate and servo together and inspect from UNDERNEATH, using a light. Compare each small printed hole with the rounded center of the servo mounting slot above it. Look for symmetric alignment at BOTH ends; on MG996R check all FOUR holes simultaneously.
5. Choose the label whose holes are most centered together. These small holes are visual references, not finished screw clearance holes. No new screws, pins or tools are required. Do not force screws through them.
6. Send the winning label for each servo. If adjacent options look equally good, send both labels and an underside photo of the best seated fit. If none fits, report that instead of forcing it.

The template is symmetric; servo direction does not matter. Raised labels sit outside the nominal servo footprint. If a particular servo's tab reaches a raised label and cannot sit flat, report that interference.
